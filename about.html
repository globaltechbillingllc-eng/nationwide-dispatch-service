About us

A dispatch team that works for the carrier, not the broker.

Why we do this

Owner-operators spend hours every day on the phone with brokers, refreshing load boards and filling out setup packets, time that should go to driving and resting. Nationwide Dispatch Services LLC takes that work off your hands.

We work on your side of the deal. Our job is to keep your truck moving on loads that pay what your miles are worth, on lanes that get you home when you want to be home.

How we work

You approve every load
We never book freight without your OK. You see the rate, miles and pickup details first.
Straight answers
You'll know what a load pays, what we charge and what the broker expects before you commit.
One dispatcher who knows you
You work with a dispatcher who learns your truck, your lanes and your schedule.
Coast to coast
We book loads in all 48 contiguous states, for one truck or a small fleet.

Ready for your next load?

Tell us about your truck and a dispatcher will get back to you within one business day.
