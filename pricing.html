Pricing

One flat percentage on the loads we book. No sign-up fee, no contract, no surprises.

What's included

  • Load searching and booking
  • Rate negotiation with brokers
  • Broker and shipper setup packets
  • Rate confirmations, BOLs and PODs
  • Invoicing and factoring paperwork
  • Check calls and detention follow-up
Dispatch fee 6% of the gross line haul per load Get started

How the fee works

You only pay on loads we book and you approve. The fee is a percentage of the line haul the broker pays, not including fuel surcharge or reimbursed expenses.

Example: one load
Line haul paid by the broker$3,000
Dispatch fee at 6%$180
You keep$2,820

Pricing questions

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. You sign a simple dispatch agreement before we start booking loads.

Do you charge for loads I turn down?

No. You only pay on loads you approve and we book.

Ready for your next load?

Tell us about your truck and a dispatcher will call you back within one business day.
