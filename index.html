How it works

  1. Tell us about your truck

    Fill out the sign-up form or call. We'll ask about your equipment, preferred lanes, home time and minimum rate.

  2. Send your documents

    MC authority, certificate of insurance, W-9 and a signed dispatch agreement. We use them for broker setups.

  3. Approve your loads

    We find and negotiate loads, then send them to you. Nothing is booked without your OK.

  4. Drive and get paid

    We handle check calls, paperwork and invoicing while you're on the road.

Questions carriers ask

Do I have to take every load you find?

No. We send you each load with the rate, miles and pickup details. You decide whether we book it.

Do you work with new authorities?

Yes. Some brokers require a few months of active authority, but we know which ones work with new MCs and can still keep you moving.

Are you a broker?

No. We're a dispatch service working for you, the carrier. Brokers pay you directly or through your factoring company.

What do I need to get started?

Active MC and DOT numbers, a certificate of insurance, a W-9, and your truck and trailer details.

Which states do you cover?

All 48 contiguous states, with lanes planned around where you want to run and when you want to be home.
