Dispatch services

Everything between finding a load and getting paid for it, handled by a dispatcher who knows your truck and your lanes.

Load searching and booking

We search the major load boards and our own broker contacts every day for freight that matches your equipment, lanes, weight limits and home time. You get the rate, miles, deadhead and pickup window before anything is booked.

Rate negotiation

We call brokers to push the rate per mile up and get accessorials agreed in writing: detention, layover, tarping and extra stops. You set the minimum rate you'll accept, and we stick to it.

Broker and shipper setup

We complete carrier setup packets with your MC authority, certificate of insurance and W-9, so you're approved before the load is ready to book.

Paperwork and documents

We send you each rate confirmation, collect BOLs and PODs after delivery, and keep your documents organized and easy to find.

Invoicing and factoring support

We send invoices to brokers, or submit paperwork to your factoring company, as soon as the load delivers, so payment isn't held up.

Trip planning and check calls

We plan loads around your hours of service, call ahead to shippers and receivers, handle broker check calls, and chase detention and layover pay when you're kept waiting.

Equipment we dispatch

Whatever you run, we know the freight and the brokers that move it.

Dry van

Reefer

Flatbed
